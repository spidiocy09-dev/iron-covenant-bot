# Privacy Policy — The Iron Covenant Bot

**Effective Date:** November 7, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

## 1. Introduction

The Iron Covenant Bot ("the Bot") is a Discord application designed to provide server management, automated welcome messages, member verification, gaming role selection, and community administration.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot accesses, how it is used, and what rights users have regarding their information.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on enabled features, the Bot may process Discord user IDs, usernames and display names, profile pictures, server IDs, role information, membership events, and command interactions. The Bot is not designed to request passwords, payment details, or personal contact information.

## 3. How Information Is Used

The Bot uses this information to welcome new members, display avatars and member counts, manage verification and gaming roles, create channels and categories, enforce restricted-access permissions, and respond to commands. The Bot does not use member information for targeted advertising.

## 4. Member Verification

Verification may involve processing a member's Discord user ID, role information, and interaction with the rules-acceptance system. Verification does not require passwords or sensitive personal information.

## 5. Moderation and Restricted Access

Authorized server staff may assign or remove the Exiled role, which controls access to the Shadow Realm. The Bot processes the account and role information needed for these actions.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

The Bot may process data through Discord's API. Whether information is stored persistently, and for how long, depends on the deployed configuration. The operator should retain data only as needed for the relevant feature and establish appropriate deletion procedures for any persistent records.

**Operator review required:** Before publishing, verify whether the deployed code writes logs, databases, or other persistent records, and replace this section with specific retention periods and deletion practices where applicable.

## 7. Data Sharing

The Bot does not intentionally sell personal information. Discord processes information as part of its platform. Welcome messages and other content posted to server channels may be visible to members with access to those channels. Hosting providers and authorized administrators may have access where necessary to operate the Bot.

## 8. Security

The operator should keep the bot token confidential, restrict administrative commands, limit access to stored data, and use appropriate permissions and secure hosting practices. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Age Requirements

The Bot is intended for use in accordance with Discord's applicable minimum-age requirements. It does not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children.

## 10. Privacy Requests

Users may contact the operator to ask about data stored by the Bot or request correction or deletion where applicable. Requests will be handled according to applicable law and the Bot's technical capabilities. Removing the Bot does not automatically erase messages previously posted in Discord channels.

## 11. Third-Party Services

The Bot uses Discord's platform and API. Discord's independent data practices are described in the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## 12. Policy Changes

The operator may update this policy as the Bot's functionality, data practices, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published in the Bot's official GitHub repository.

## 13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Bot operator through the official Discord server or a contact method published in the Bot's GitHub repository.
